About me

I build the thing and I run the thing — front end, back end, and the infrastructure underneath it.

Elyotam Cohen

I build websites, web apps and online stores, and I automate the parts of a business that should not need a person sitting in front of them. Most of that work goes out through CODE92, where the brief is usually the same: something that loads fast, reads well in Hebrew, and does not fall over.

The other half of what I do is infrastructure. Containers, orchestration and infrastructure as code — Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, Terraform, Ansible and Argo CD on AWS, with Prometheus and Grafana watching it. I like the part of the job where a deploy stops being a ceremony and becomes a push.

Lately most of my time goes into AI automation: wiring models into real workflows so that content, data handling and the glue between tools happens on its own. The AI coding tools in the stack below are not decoration — they are how the work actually gets done now.
